Fluent-Qt 1.9.3 improves window background stability during startup and
material changes. It also fixes window captures that could finish a material
change before the window itself had drawn its replacement background.

## Window background fixes

- New windows prepare their theme and material background before becoming
  visible. This avoids a brief white or mismatched background in dark mode
  and when Mica or Acrylic uses a painted fallback on Windows, macOS and
  Linux.
- Switching from Mica or Acrylic to Normal (`BackdropEffect::Solid`), or to
  a painted fallback, keeps the previous native material until the window
  has drawn and submitted its opaque replacement. The change waits while
  drawing is paused, and a recreated window must paint its background afresh.
- Widget screenshots and cache refreshes no longer count as a real window
  redraw or finish an in-progress material change. Captures are handled
  safely both before the first paint and after the window is shown.
